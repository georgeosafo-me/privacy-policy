Privacy Policy - Church Management App

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Church Management Application ("the Service"), developed by George Osafo (Dosagel), processes organizational and member data.

1. Information Handled

The application assists local church administrators in managing member registries, event attendance, and ministry contributions.

  • Member Records: Names, contact numbers, and attendance records are entered directly by authorized church administrators.
  • Access Control: Data is restricted to verified church personnel using role-based authentication.
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, rent, or share congregation records with third-party marketers or advertisers.

2. Data Security

Administrative communications and database transfers use industry-standard TLS encryption. Backups are stored in secure cloud environments with strict identity management.
