Privacy Policy

ProKey Keyboard

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 • Version: 1.0

Core Privacy Commitment: ProKey is designed with a strict local-first philosophy. Your keystrokes, personal typing data, passwords, and clipboard content remain on your physical device and are never sold, collected, or uploaded to our servers.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ProKey Keyboard ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our Android keyboard application. We believe your typing habits and personal communications belong exclusively to you.

2. Information We Process & How It Is Handled

A. Keystrokes & Text Input (Local Only)

  • Processing: All predictive text, autocomplete suggestions, multi-word completions, swipe gestures, and grammar corrections are calculated locally using on-device neural language models and binary dictionaries.
  • Storage: Words you type or add to your personal dictionary are stored solely in your device's private, encrypted app sandbox. We do not transmit or log what you type.

B. Clipboard History (Local Only)

  • Processing: ProKey provides an optional on-device clipboard history tool so you can reuse recently copied snippets and images.
  • Storage: Clipboard items are stored exclusively in local device storage. You can delete individual clips or clear your entire clipboard history at any time.

C. Voice Input / Speech-to-Text

  • Permission: RECORD_AUDIO
  • Usage: When you tap the microphone button, voice audio is processed in real time by Android's built-in speech recognition framework to convert spoken words into text.
  • Storage: ProKey never records, saves, or stores your voice files.

D. Contact Suggestions (Optional)

  • Permission: READ_CONTACTS (Optional)
  • Usage: If explicitly granted, ProKey references contact names solely to provide autocomplete suggestions when you type a name.
  • Storage: Contact information is processed locally in memory and is never uploaded, synced, or shared with any external server.

E. Internet-Connected Features

  • Permission: INTERNET
  • Math Formula Rendering: When you choose to export LaTeX equations as high-resolution images or GIFs, the equation string is sent via HTTPS to CodeCogs' rendering API to generate the formula graphic. No personal identifying information is attached.
  • AI Typing Assistance (Optional): If you explicitly trigger an AI query or assistant feature in the keyboard toolbar, your specific prompt text is transmitted via secure HTTPS to the designated AI API to return the requested answer or text transformation.

F. Device Haptics

  • Permission: VIBRATE
  • Usage: Used exclusively to deliver tactile haptic feedback when you press keys or drag the navigation joystick.

3. Third-Party Services & Links

ProKey does not incorporate third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking SDKs, or data brokers. The only external services utilized are on-demand APIs invoked by user action:

  • Google Play Services: For application licensing, distribution, and in-app updates.
  • LaTeX Rendering API (CodeCogs): For generating equation images when you tap the IMG/GIF export buttons.
  • AI Language APIs (e.g., Google Gemini): Only when you explicitly use the AI feature.

4. Data Retention & User Control

You have full ownership and control over all data stored by ProKey:

  • Clear Personal Dictionary: Go to ProKey Settings → Prediction → Reset Dictionary to erase all learned words and overrides.
  • Clear Clipboard: Tap the "Wipe" / trash icon in the Clipboard panel to clear your clip history.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can grant or revoke Microphone, Contacts, and Storage permissions anytime in Android System Settings → Apps → ProKey → Permissions.
  • Full Deletion: Uninstalling the app permanently purges all local databases, cached images, and user preferences from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

ProKey does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Since all input processing occurs locally on the device, no personal data from minors is gathered or stored externally.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security safeguards. Your data is protected by Android's application sandboxing architecture, ensuring other installed applications cannot access your keyboard buffer, personal dictionary, or clipboard history without your consent.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
