Privacy Policy - STEMFinite School Platform

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how the STEMFinite / School Management System ("the Platform"), developed by George Osafo (Dosagel), handles institutional and student information.

1. Educational Information Handling

The platform is designed to assist educational institutions in record-keeping and student learning progress.

  • Student & Academic Records: Course registrations, attendance, and assessment grades are managed under school administration oversight.
  • Student Privacy & FERPA/GDPR Compliance: Student data is never commercialized, sold, or used for behavioral advertising.
  • Teacher & Parent Access: Access to academic evaluations is strictly partitioned by institutional role.

2. Data Security

All transmitted information is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS 1.3, ensuring high-security standard protection for sensitive student educational data.
